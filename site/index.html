Screenshots at the speed of hjkl

Vishot is a keyboard-first, Vim-inspired screenshot tool for macOS. Pick a window, a single button, or a whole display, mark it up, and paste it without reaching for the mouse.

How it works

  1. ⇧⌘S opens the picker. From there it's hjkl, Tab and ↵. The mouse can stay where it is.

  2. hjkl or the arrow keys jump to the nearest window in that direction, across every screen.

  3. f puts a label on every button, message and pane. Type the label and it's selected. No dragging a box around it.

  4. vishot capture --app Chat saves a PNG and prints its path, so a coding agent can look at the app it's building. A bundled skill teaches the agent the commands.

  5. ⇧↵ freezes the shot in place. a draws an arrow, r a box, t a label, c crops.

  6. y copies it, s saves a PNG to your screenshot folder, S does both.

Try it here: j k or 1–6

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature on this page is free: picking, labels, markup, copy and save. There may be optional paid extras later, but nothing here will move behind a paywall.

When can I try it?

No date yet. Join the waitlist and you'll get one email when the beta opens.

How is it different from the built-in screenshots?

The built-in tool makes you click a window or drag a box around what you want. Vishot picks windows, single buttons and panes, or whole displays from the keyboard, and lets you mark the shot up before it's copied.

Do I need to know Vim?

No. It's Vim-inspired, not Vim-only. The arrow keys, Tab and ↵ do the same as hjkl, and the bar at the bottom of the screen always shows the keys you can press. The mouse works too: hover selects, click copies.

Can I change the keys?

Yes. By release every key will be remappable in Settings, the ⇧⌘S shortcut included, so you can make it feel like Vim, like Emacs, or like neither.

What permissions does it need?

Screen Recording, to take the screenshot. Accessibility is optional: it powers the f labels, and Vishot asks for it the first time you press f, never at launch.

Does it work in Chrome, Slack and Electron apps?

Yes. The first time you press f in one of them, the labels take a few seconds to appear while the app builds its accessibility tree. After that they're quick.

Where do my screenshots go?

To the clipboard, or as a timestamped PNG in your macOS screenshot folder (the Desktop unless you changed it). ⌘S in the editor lets you pick the place.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. Vishot makes no network requests. Every capture stays on your clipboard or your disk.
