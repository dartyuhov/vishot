How it works

  1. Shift⌘S opens the picker. Pick a window, select an element, mark it up and copy it without touching the mouse. hjkl or the arrow keys steer. The mouse works too, but it doesn't have to.

  2. The arrow keys jump to the nearest window in that direction, across every screen. G takes a whole display, and v selects any rectangle.

  3. f puts a label on every button, message and pane. Type the label and it's selected. No dragging a box around it.

  4. Shift↵ freezes the shot in place. a draws an arrow, r a box, t a label, c crops.

  5. History preview: release availability is not yet verified. Open Capture History from Vishot’s menu bar. hl or Left/Right selects an earlier screenshot; y copies it again and closes history.

  6. y copies it, s saves a PNG to your screenshot folder, S does both. Nothing is uploaded: Vishot makes no network requests.

  7. —CLI Coming soon

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature on this page is free: picking, labels, markup, copy and save. There may be optional paid extras later, but nothing here will move behind a paywall.

How do I install Vishot?

Vishot requires macOS 14 or later. In Apple menu → About This Mac, “Chip” with an Apple M-series name means Apple Silicon; “Processor” with an Intel name means Intel. Choose the matching DMG, open it, drag Vishot into Applications, then eject the disk image and launch Vishot from Applications.

macOS says it can’t verify the app. What now?

After trying to open Vishot, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, find the message about Vishot and choose Open Anyway. Confirm Open and authenticate if asked. This approves Vishot individually; normal macOS security checks still apply to both DMG and Homebrew installs.

How do I update it?

For a direct install, quit Vishot, download the newer DMG for your chip and replace Vishot in Applications, then eject the image and reopen the app. For a Homebrew install, run brew update then brew upgrade --cask vishot. There is no automatic in-app updater.

How is it different from the built-in screenshots?

The built-in tool makes you click a window or drag a box around what you want. Vishot picks windows, single buttons and panes, or whole displays from the keyboard, and lets you mark the shot up before it's copied.

Do I need to know Vim?

No. It's Vim-inspired, not Vim-only. The arrow keys, Tab and ↵ do the same as hjkl, and the bar at the bottom of the screen always shows the keys you can press. The mouse works too: hover selects, click copies.

Can I change the keys?

You can change the global capture shortcut in Vishot’s Settings; the default is Shift⌘S. The picker and editor’s internal keys are fixed, not individually remappable.

What permissions does it need?

Allow Screen Recording in System Settings → Privacy & Security so Vishot can capture your screen. Accessibility is optional: it powers the f labels and bringing selected windows forward. Enable it there when you want those features. If macOS asks you to quit and reopen Vishot after a permission change, do so.

Does it work in Chrome, Slack and Electron apps?

Yes. The first time you press f in one of them, the labels take a few seconds to appear while the app builds its accessibility tree. After that they're quick.

Where do my screenshots go?

To the clipboard, or as a timestamped PNG in your macOS screenshot folder (the Desktop unless you changed it). ⌘S in the editor lets you pick the place.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. Vishot makes no network requests. Every capture stays on your clipboard or your disk.
